A function $f$ is $K$ **Lipschitz** if:
$$
|f(x_1)-f(x_2)| \leq K\|x_1-x_2\|
$$
for every pair $x_1, x_2$.

This says that a small change in the input cannot produce an arbitrarily large change in the output.

For a differentiable scalar function, a sufficient local interpretation is:
$$
\|\nabla_x f(x)\|_2\leq K
$$
